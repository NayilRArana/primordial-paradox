(`data/scenes/all.json:20`). `quay_morgan_pre` is three pages, so the say needs two presses a page, six in all, and
`pp_talk_persona.gml:633` says the answer is "drawn until the player confirms", so the leg needs exactly ONE more press
after the answer lands.

`data/demo/run_002.json` held seven rows there instead: `r020` complete the typing of a choice page, `r021` Down to
`Crew list.`, `r022` Confirm on it, and `r023` to `r026` four presses for "the bluff reply, two pages". Every one of
those is a page of `quay_gate_checkpoint`, which the quay only story deleted, so a take was pressing at boxes that
cannot open and then spending 1.8 s of its clock doing it.

Changed: `r020` is now one `confirm` gated on `PP: talk scene answer, npc morgan` (written at
`pp_talk_persona.gml:672`), `marker_delay_steps` 30, `marker_wait_s` 15. The wait is 15 s because the script's own
`talk.timeout_s` is 12: a late live answer holds the tape instead of being pressed past. `r021` to `r026` are gone.

Pinned: `tests/test_demo_shots.py::test_the_gate_leg_presses_the_talk_box_and_no_deleted_choice` reads the scene's own
command list, the dialogue's own page count, the script's own timeout and the GML's own log literal, so the press count
cannot drift from the scene and a deleted page cannot come back into the leg.
