## 3. What the overlay draws, and what it does not

Draws: the six captions, verbatim from the rewritten script, in the band `tools/video_builder.py` declares for
each shot; and the network line over S5 at 101.00 to 104.00 s, which is the script's own window (section 5).

Does not draw: the three bake receipts. `data/meta_bake.json` holds Morgan's three `fight` rows
(`fight/morgan/1`, `/2`, `/3`) as `source: fallback`, `network: false`, so `tools/demo_overlay.py` refuses to
draw them as live answers, which is the script's fallback 1 in section 2: "S5 draws its caption and its box and
no plate". The windows are declared anyway (83.40 to 87.40, 91.00 to 95.00, 98.00 to 101.00 s) and printed in
the verify file under `receipts_declared_not_drawn`, so the moment the Q1 re-bake lands the same build draws
them with no other change.

Also not drawable today: the NES talk box, for two independent reasons, both of which the tool names. The row
must be a live Meta answer (it is fallback), and even a live row needs a verdict receipt in
`data/receipts/meta_receipts.jsonl`, which no `fight/morgan` row has. `tools/render_talk_box_still.py` therefore
refuses with the fix named (`tools\receipts.py --row fight/morgan/1`), and its default shot is now S5.
