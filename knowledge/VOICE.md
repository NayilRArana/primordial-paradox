# Primordial Paradox comic voice

Target: a deadpan supernatural comedy. Never stack jokes until the scene forgets what is at stake.

## The 12 rules

1. Be specific and totally serious. "The deck tilts. The mop does not." beats random silliness.
2. Every inspectable object gets one useful joke rooted in its job: a bed, kettle, sign, bin, or shop window.
3. One idea per box, at most three short lines. Put the funny word last; cut setup that only explains setup.
4. Give people small wants during disasters. A sailor can fear the sea and still want his parking validated.
5. Make divinity meet administration. Zeus commands storms, not the halls laundry card.
6. Make myth jokes work twice. Konrad stealing snacks is a dad joke first, Kronos eating his children second.
7. After the jokes, allow one sincere beat. Then cut away. Never explain why it mattered.
8. Running gags take three beats: establish, repeat, twist. Snacks vanish, return, then become evidence.
9. Use cause and effect. Because the gate opens, the clerk runs out of excuses; never merely "and then".
10. Give key choices distinct voices and real consequences. Politeness, bluff, and authority must not share a reply.
11. Southampton is home, not a punchline. Use Portswood, the Itchen, Red Funnel, students, and rain with affection.
12. Read every box aloud. If the rhythm sags or the joke needs a footnote, cut it.

## Character rules

Zeus (Zander):
1) Ask practical questions.
2) Stay calm at miracles, but lose to ordinary admin.
3) Let his cloud reveal what he will not say.

Kronos (Konrad):
1) Sound courteous even when cruel.
2) Deliver insults as assessments, never shouting.
3) Turn appetite and parental concern into quiet menace.

Lt. Percy:
1) Project every sentence.
2) Put procedure before fear or pity.
3) Translate sea-god grandeur into watches, forms, and mops.

Ouranos (Urien):
1) Be warm and chatty.
2) Use affectionate, dated language.
3) Treat weather like former property and blades like bad memories.

## 12 example lines

1. The deck tilts. The mop does not.
2. Laundry card: DECLINED.
3. Your father calls this packing. The biscuits call it theft.
4. Put the knife away, lad. Bad memories.
5. I used to own the weather. Terrible overheads.
6. Can a hurricane validate parking?
7. No pass, no gate. The kettle has a pass.
8. The Itchen is rising. The queue is not.
9. This storm feels like home. Home was louder.
10. The last ferry is held. The tea is leaving anyway.
11. You could eat me up. Finish the crisps first.
12. Form 6B permits one miracle per customer.
