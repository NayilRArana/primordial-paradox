# Character bible v1 (Nayil's directions, 2026-10-21)

Setting: REAL LIFE mixed with Greek myth. Modern Britain, ordinary clothes and jobs; the gods show through as powers, auras and "god form" looks. Nobody wears ancient armour in a modern scene (Nayil: "not just yeeting a guy in ancient armor inside a Navy ship").
Naming rule: all four share the surname **Skye**; each first name starts with the letter of their god: Kronos K, Zeus Z, Poseidon P, Hades H.

| God | Reincarnation (proposal, Nayil can change) | Letter | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kronos | **Konrad Skye** | K | Konrad means "bold counsel": the Titan who counselled and then devoured; alt Keir, Kieran |
| Zeus | **Zander Skye** | Z | Zander, "defender of men"; Zeus is the defender and the sky; alt Zephyr, Zane |
| Poseidon | **Percy Skye** | P | Percy kept: Percival and the sea; the nickname fits the Navy officer; alt Perran (Cornish, "little Piran", a sea county) |
| Hades | **Hadrian Skye** | H | Hadrian is a Roman emperor's name and a near echo of Hades; alt Hale, Hollis |

Family: the Skyes. Konrad is the father of Zander (19), Percy (about 24) and Hadrian (about 30)? (Ages are Nayil's: Zander first year student, Percy early to mid 20s, Hadrian late 20s to early 30s, Konrad 40s. A father in his 40s with a late 20s son is possible only as a young father or by the myth's own logic: the siblings are reincarnations, not necessarily blood relatives. Treat the Skye surname as the shared mark of the cycle. Flagged for Nayil.)

## Konrad Skye (Kronos), 40s
Handsome. Research scientist. Long blond hair like Zander's, streaked with white and grey, tied back or loose. A well shaped beard, trimmed close to the face, not bushy. Outfit: smart casual research scientist (open lab coat or a charcoal overcoat over a shirt and knitted tie, ID lanyard). Tells: checks a wristwatch; a sickle shaped keyring. God form: Titan of time, hourglass sands and a harvest sickle.
## Zander Skye (Zeus), 19
Close to the current concept (Nayil approves): messy blond hair, looks like a first year student. Jacket: oversized bomber in LIGHT BLUE (not green). Earbuds. Static sparks from the hair; a small storm cloud follows him. God form: thunderbolt, white blue light.
## Percy Skye (Poseidon), early to mid 20s
Stockier. Royal Navy Lieutenant in the modern uniform (navy jacket with the lieutenant's rank stripes, white shirt, dark tie or working dress), fuller beard, ginger to brown hair. Holds a TRIDENT. A sea coloured aura (teal water light, spray) to show he is being taken over or is using Poseidon's power. No knight armour. The boss fight happens inside a Navy ship (grey steel corridors, bulkheads, a wardroom).
## Hadrian Skye (Hades), late 20s to early 30s
Data centre CEO. Sleek, handsome, looks like he has not seen sunlight (pale, dark circles). Black tailored suit adorned with gems (gem tie pin, cufflinks, studs, a ring). God form: a BIDENT (two pronged spear) and the HELM OF INVISIBILITY. Setting: server halls, cold blue light, cables.

## Leitmotifs (manager, 2026-10-21; Nayil: every god needs one)
| Character | Motif | Shape | Key and meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Konrad (Kronos) | K | E5 C5 D5 E5, dotted quarter then three stepwise eighths | A minor, the family call; heard in every theme |
| Zander (Zeus) | Z | G4 C5 E5, a rising major arpeggio in three quarter notes | C major, sunny, simple |
| Percy (Poseidon) | P, the tide | E4 G4 B4 rising then E5 D5 B4 crest and fall; three notes like three prongs | E minor (v of A minor); the boss theme moves it to G minor, 3/4 |
| Hadrian (Hades) | H, the bident | two descending half steps D5 C#5, then a fourth lower A4 G#4, with silence between | D minor (iv); cold, exact |
Rule: each motif appears in its owner's cue and as a short quotation in the others (the overture states all four). Variation operations per cue: inversion, augmentation, minor or major swap, and key change; never rewrite the shape so it cannot be recognised.
