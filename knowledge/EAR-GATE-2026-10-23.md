### The four gate axes

The card asks for four axes. These are the four, taken from the sweep's own ceiling analysis: the sweep's 17
files never scored hook above 7 or variety above 8, so those two are at their ceiling and cannot order the
slice, while drums (ceiling 5), tension (6), human (4) and structure (7) are where the sweep says the headroom
is. Hook and variety are still recorded in the table, and are not gated.

| axis | what a fix has to change | ceiling in the 17 file sweep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| drums | the kit: pattern id, rung length, fill id, ghost level | 5 |
| tension | harmonic colour: an altered chord, a stab, a pedal | 6 |
| human | note length and gate, velocity steps, vibrato events | 4 |
| structure | section contrast, a hole before a return, level per section | 7 |

### One concrete fix per cue, with its measure

The measure is the thing a builder can type into a score file, not a description of a feeling.

1. **title PP-01, tension 4.** Add the tritone stab MET-08 on beat 4 of bar 20 only, one per bar at most: root and
   flat fifth on the two pulses, one beat, 4 frame decay. Measure: one new chord event, `bar 20 beat 4`.
2. **crawl.** Same master and same fix as row 1; no separate work, and the row exists so the slice order is complete.
3. **drive PP-02, human 3.** The score declares vibrato on every lead note of three eighths or longer, and the
   delivered MIDI carries **0** vibrato events: `pp_drive_v2.mid` has 2928 note ons, 17 distinct velocities, and
   zero controller 1 and zero controller 20 events, where `pp_03_harbor.mid` writes 106 of each for the same lead.
   Write the vibrato pair on the lead. Measure: controller 1 and 20 event count 0 to at least 106.
4. **arrival and gate PP-03, drums 4.** Move the home pattern from `CLM-01` to `CLM-03` in sections one and three
   and halve the variation rung from 8 bars to 4, so a rung changes twice as often inside one 16 bar section.
   Measure: `home: CLM-01` to `home: CLM-03`, and the `rungs` array from 8 entries to 4 per section.
5. **ship PP-05, human 1.** The score gives pulse 1 no notes at all for the whole cue (`score_pp_05.yaml`, pulse_1
   role: "silent for the whole cue") and the lead rests in 15 of 24 bars; the MIDI confirms the thinness with only
   3 distinct velocities across 611 notes. Add a 4 bar counterline on pulse 1 over bars 17 to 20 at level 5 and
   raise the noise from level 3 to level 6. Measure: notes 611 to about 900, `level: 3` to `level: 6` on noise.
6. **first fight PP-04, drums 4.** Put a one bar fill from the library into the tail bar: `FLF-01` in bar 29, plus
   ghost notes at level 2 on the offbeat eighths of bars 5 to 8. Measure: fill id `FLF-01`, one bar, noise level 2.
7. **game over, human 3.** The sting is 1.4 s and its held last note has no shape. Hold the tonic 0.9 s and start a
   delayed vibrato 3 frames after the attack (HOK-28) instead of a straight tone. Measure: last note 0.9 s, vibrato
   delay 3 frames.
8. **save jingle, human 3.** Gate the final note to 70 percent and rest the last eighth (HOK-30, breath rest at the

## 4. The two loudest claims, re-read against the rendered file's own measurement

**Claim one: the ship cue is the floor of the slice, and its floor is real thinness rather than a bad mood.**
Re-read: `assets/audio/pp_05_small_ship/measure_pp_05.json`, the cue's own measurement, reads 24 bars with
section RMS of 3785.0 for section A, 4018.8 for A_fugue, 5414.3 for A_second and 3766.8 for the close, so the
three non climax sections sit within 6.2 percent of each other and the whole cue's bar to bar spread is 41.71
percent, with the quietest bar at -20.05 dBFS and the loudest at -15.37 dBFS. `ffmpeg` volumedetect on the
rendered mp3 puts it at -18.1 dB mean, the lowest of the seven files today. The score says the same thing in
words: pulse 1 is silent for the whole cue and the lead rests in 15 of 24 bars. The measurement agrees with the
claim. It also shows why the two judges disagreed by 14 points: the ear is hearing a cue that is genuinely one
texture for 60 s, and a harsher listener calls that a 15, a kinder one a 29.

**Claim two: the human axis is not stuck because vibrato is missing from the renderer, it is stuck because it is
missing from some cues.**
Re-read against the delivered MIDI, which is what the renderer is fed: `title_theme_v5.mid` carries 137 vibrato
events (controller 1 and controller 20) over 1087 note ons and 102 distinct velocities, `pp_03_harbor.mid` 106
over 1375 notes, `pp_04_navy_sailors.mid` 158 over 1253 notes, but `pp_drive_v2.mid` carries **zero** vibrato
events over 2928 notes and `pp_05_small_ship.mid` carries zero over 611 notes with only 3 distinct velocities.
The drive's own score declares vibrato in words ("vibrato on notes of three eighths and longer", section A), but
`tools/drive_v2.py` has no vibrato code at all and the score has no voice block to carry a depth, so score and
render disagree, and that is a build gap rather than a taste call. The measurement agrees with the claim, and it
narrows the human fix from "humanize everything" to "write the events the score already promises on the drive,
and give the ship a performance to shape".

  the MIDI, not against the ear's description of them.
- The two stingers are 1.4 s and about 1 s. Four of the six rubric questions (structure, variety, tension, drums)
  barely mean anything at that length, so their scores are only useful next to each other, and the fix there is
  a note length and a gate rather than a rewrite.
- Nothing here proves a cue is good. It proves where the queue is.

- Rows: 8, one per cue in the slice, each naming its own file. `ls assets/audio/ear_reviews/*20261023T02*.md`
  lists the reviews the rows come from.
- Title: `sha256sum assets/audio/pp_01_title/pp_01_title.mp3 assets/audio/title_theme_v5/title_theme_v5.mp3`

- Scores: `python tools/ear_critic.py --ledger` and read the summary rows dated `2026-10-23`, model
  `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`. Each summary row carries the six scores used above.
- Style bible: `python tools/check_music_style_bible.py` ends with `RESULT: PASS`.
- Levels: `ffmpeg -i <file> -af volumedetect -f null NUL` on the seven files named in the table.
- MIDI claims: count controller 1 and controller 20 events in the five cue MIDI files; the drive and the ship
  return zero.
- Dashes: the file carries no em dash, en dash, figure dash, horizontal bar or minus sign, the same rule
  `check_music_style_bible.py` C4 enforces on the bible.
