# MAP-AUDIT

Card LD-2. Every room in data/maps/all.json measured against the walkability rules, with the evidence that
says why the current maps feel janky. Generated by tools/map_audit.py; nothing here changes a room, a tile or
a collision, those fixes are card LD-3.

## Method

Each room gets a walkability grid from its collision layer, then six checks:

1. Unreachable walkable tiles: flood fill from every entrance over 4 connectivity; any walkable tile the fill
   does not reach is floor the player can see but never stand on (playbook rule 4a).
2. Single tile gaps and pinch corridors: walkable tiles with fewer than two neighbours, or exactly two on one
   axis (a one cell wide corridor), plus dead ends (rule 4b).
3. Snag corners: a walker bot (tools/walker_bot.py) presses a diagonal into every concave corner and reports
   the corners where the wall slide sticks, i.e. the 3 px nudge cannot clear the lip (rule 4c).
4. Dead ends without a reward: a dead end with no point of interest on it or beside it.
5. Doors and transitions that do not match on both sides (rule 5).
6. Longest walk without a point of interest, BFS from every trigger and actor (rule 1).

The score ranks rooms by total problem weight; a higher number is more janky. The overlay marks reachable, blocked,
unreachable, snag and dead end tiles.
