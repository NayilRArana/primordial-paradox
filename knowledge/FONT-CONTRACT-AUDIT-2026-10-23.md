## A3. What the two tests actually assert, and the one hole

`tests/test_make_title_ui.py:173` reads frames 74, 8x8, image 592x8 from a sheet regenerated into a throwaway root,
then `tests/test_make_title_ui.py:200` compares that regenerated sheet frame by frame to `pressstart2p_font.bitmap`.
`tests/test_nes_check.py:220` runs the NES sprite audit on the committed png and reads 592x8, 74 tiles, 1 colour.

So both read the same three numbers (74, 8x8, 592x8), which is what the card asked for. The hole: the pixel identity
to Press Start 2P is asserted only for the regenerated copy, and `tools/check_font_coverage.py:140` (`read_sheet`)
checks the committed sheet's geometry, blank frames and ink but never compares its pixels to the rasteriser. A
committed sheet that drifted in pixels while keeping 74 non blank one colour frames would pass every existing check.
Measured today it has not drifted: 0 of 74 frames differ (A1). The hole is real but currently empty.
