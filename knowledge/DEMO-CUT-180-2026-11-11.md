## 3. Audio, and what it is not

The captures that carry audio keep their own audio: `demo-talk`, `quay`, `doors`, `morgan_talk`. The two that
carry none are `first_fight_talk_capture.mp4` (its own verify: "no audio stream at all, PP muted") and
`save_capture.mp4`. Under those the game's own cue files are placed, at the seconds the script's Music column
names: `assets\audio\pp_04_navy_sailors\pp_04_navy_sailors.ogg` for the encounter and the TALK fight, and
`assets\audio\sfx\save_jingle.ogg` under the save.

So the track is not a loopback capture of the game's output, and this file does not claim it is. The stand-in bed
`demo-talk` carries is already recorded as a stand-in in its own verify file ("rendered stand-in from
demo-talk-2026-10-26_audio.m4a, not the game's live output").

## 4. What the cut shows, and what it does not

The seven things a judge is asked to follow are all on screen in order: title, crawl, the night quay, the doors,
the talk at the table with its options and its spare, the walk with the text box, the TALK fight through three
turns to SPARE, and the save. Three of the script's six scenes are not in that list, and they are not on screen:

- **the drive (S3)**: no capture of the drive exists in this repository. `knowledge/DEMO-SCRIPT.md` S3 says so
  itself (the drive block of `data/scenes/all.json` was never written) and the 2026-10-26 cut note measured it:
  "the drive: not in any recording".
- **the dock office, the boarding pass and the boarding (S5)**: `quay_dock_office` is in `data/dialogue/all.json`
  but no capture of it on a quiet desktop exists, and the ship story was cut from the game on 2026-11-01.
- **the gate checkpoint and its three choices (S4)**: retired with the same cut; `quay_gate_checkpoint` is not in
  the build, and Morgan's talk carries the same claim: three answers, no failure state, the way opens.

The captions are the script's own words, verbatim, because the script owns them. Two of the six therefore describe
a scene that is not the one under them (S3's "one of twelve drive shots", S5's "the pass is one page and one
flag"), and that is a real mismatch. This note is the record of it, and section 2 is the table a reader checks it
against. Burning neither caption would have been a smaller lie and a larger hole: the card asks for the six.

## 5. Q: for gamedev, one question

**Which is the authority for what the demo shows, `knowledge/DEMO-SCRIPT.md` (2026-10-22, six shots with the
drive, the dock office and the ship) or the story the build now has (commit 28d407a2, the quay only story: title,
crawl, hurricane Town Quay, Captain Morgan, Percy, two credits endings)?** The shot table cannot be filmed as
written, because three of its six scenes are not in the build. If the quay only story wins, S3, S4 and S5 should be
rewritten to the scenes that exist and the cut above is the picture for it; if the shot table wins, the drive, the
office and the boarding have to be built and captured before any 180 s cut can be a recording of them. This lane
does not decide it: gamedev's specs are never overridden, and the captions belong to whoever authors the script.

## 6. The command, and what is still missing

```
cd "<repo>"
python tools\demo_cut_180.py --plan          # the segment table, the captions, the provenance, nothing written
python tools\demo_cut_180.py --build         # writes output\demo\cut-180-2026-11-11.mp4 (about 70 s on this PC)
python tools\demo_cut_180.py --verify        # writes the _verify.json beside it and prints every row
```

What this cut is not: it is not a recorded take. A recorded take is the one thing that would replace it, and it
needs a quiet desktop (`python tools\play_demo.py --shot-table full`, which refuses while `codex.exe` runs) and the
route's own reds in `tests\test_demo_shots.py` to be green. Neither is this lane's to clear. Until then, this file
is the best 180.00 s picture that exists, every second of it measured, every substitution named.
