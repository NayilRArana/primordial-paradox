## What the music does

One two bar cell, stated in every unit:

    bar 1 (rise):  A4 B4 (C5 D5) E5 held        stepwise rise to a held peak, then the peak again
    bar 2 (fall):  E5 D5 C5 B4 A4 E4              stepwise fall, the one leap (A4 to E4, 7 semitones)

The third statement of each four bar pair (bars 5, 13, 21) raises the peak to A5 and answers on
C5, so the third return is 83 percent the same rather than a copy. Every eighth bar takes a
different drum figure, every fourth bar a different one again, and the eighth bar of each block
takes the fill (the card's rule). The last four units erase the head of the cell, which is the
cue's brief, and the final bar holds one tonic A4 over a whole bar triangle pedal.

Chromatic budget: five melody notes of 167, 3.0 percent, inside the 3 to 6 percent band of
`knowledge/MUSIC-DISSONANCE.md`. All five are D-sharp appoggiaturas a semitone under E, prepared
by the G-sharp before them, resolving up by step into E over the E chord (bars 12, 20, 24, 28,
30). None sits on the first or last note of a statement (F1) and none is in the cadence bar 32
(F2).

Two seam experiments are worth recording, because they cost two builds:

1. A whole bar melody note at the loop end reads as a dip. Each note in `tools/render_chip.py`
   gets a linear decay to zero at its own end, so notes that stop on the bar line are silent
   exactly at the seam (first build: tail -55 dB in the last 10 ms).
2. Retriggering the melody and the pad late in the last bar helps a little (-47 dB) but does not
   close it. The triangle voice is the only one with no decay envelope, so a whole bar tonic
   pedal under the last bar is what makes the seam clean. That is the version shipped here.
