# PP-DRIVE, "The Drive": the cue for the London to Southampton film. Card MUS-CUE-DRIVE (t_0c5ef0c5)

Written 2026-10-22 for Primordial Paradox, musician lane. Original throughout; nothing quoted from a
published game. Technique only, from `knowledge/MUSIC-STYLE-BIBLE.md`, `knowledge/CHARACTER-BIBLE.md`
(the four leitmotifs) and `knowledge/MUSIC-HOOK-GRAMMAR.md`.

The picture this scores is `knowledge/DRIVE-SHOT-LIST.md`: twelve shots, 178.00 s, eight locations,
hard cuts only on a musical downbeat. Palette first (ART-BIBLE RULE 0) applies to scene art, not to
this file; the palette note below is what the music is matched to, in the same spirit.

## 1. What the cue is

- 125 bars of 3/4 at 126 bpm on the `data/title_timing.json` grid: one bar is 85.714 steps, 87 steps
  as the integer the frame counters run on, 1.4286 s. 125 bars = 178.571 s.
- The picture is 178.00 s. The cue runs 0.571 s past the last fade on purpose: the tonic is held
  through the fade to black, which is what a cue does, and the shot list says shot 12 ends on the
  fade rather than on a cut, so no bar has to be sacrificed to hit 178.00 exactly.
- Three sections, three stretches of road, each with its own palette of voices:

| Section | Bars | Length | Film it covers | Key and surface |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 1 to 25 | 35.71 s | shots 1 to 3: Big Ben, the street, the gantry sign 55 | A minor. Sparse: Alberti wheels, oom pah bodywork, no kick, hats on 2 and 3 |
| B | 26 to 59 | 48.57 s | shots 4 to 6: rain, the radio pages, the sign 43 | C major. Motor eighths: walking triangle, hats on every beat, lead duty 0.5 |
| A_return | 60 to 125 | 94.29 s | shots 7 to 12: the cutting, the cathedral, sign 30, the storm, sign 20, the docks | E minor then A minor. Full band: kick on beat 1 from bar 60, echo, fill at 125 |

Loops: A loops in place (bars 1 to 25), B loops in place (bars 26 to 59), A_return does not loop
because it ends the film. The services interlude (28 bars in the shot list) walks over B's loop, so
the cue does not repeat for it; that is why the cue is 125 bars and not the film's 152.6 wall bars.

- Loop json written by the renderer: 3/4, 126 bpm, eight voice map, source duration 178.563 s.
- Hook grammar (`hook_score`, on the melody as arranged): 46 of 100. Weakest criterion is motif
  coverage at 13 percent (4.4 of 20 points), then steps over leaps at 0 percent of 3 sampled
  intervals. Both are the same fact: this lead is arpeggios and leaps because a road cue's melody is
  the wheels, and the cheap fix (smoothing it to steps) would make 178 s of motorway into a lullaby.
  Recorded here as a measured weakness and a deliberate decision, not as a pass.

## 5. The ear loop: not run, and why

`tools/ear_critic.py --dry-run` on the mp3 reports `key: absent from GEMINI_API_KEY, a real run would
refuse`. No Gemini key is present in this session's environment (checked with `env` for names only:
GEMINI/GOOGLE keys are not set; the ones that are set are unrelated tokens). No key file was opened
and none was printed, per the card. So the humanize and the ear score are half done: the humanized
render exists and is measured above, and the Gemini ear review is the one item this card could not
run in its 45 minutes. It is one command once a session with the key is available:

    python tools/ear_critic.py --model gemini-3.5-flash assets/audio/pp_drive/pp_drive.mp3

and then the rotation (gemini-3.5-pro, then the medium and spicy variants logged in
`assets/audio/ear_reviews/` by the title theme lane) against `pp_drive_humanized.mp3`.

## 6. Q: for gamedev, and what this file does not do

- `Q1` (registration) The drive has no cue number. `data/audio.json` has PP-01 Wessex Lane, PP-02 High
  Street, PP-03 to PP-16 built or planned, and nothing for the drive. Roadmap section 4 item 3 says the
  music lane does not touch `data/audio.json` in this batch, so the asset is delivered as
  `pp_drive` (`assets/audio/pp_drive/`) and the registration is gamedev's call: a new cue id
  (PP-02b is where it sits in the intro), or a replacement for PP-02, or the shot list's own answer.
- `Q2` (one cue per film) `DRIVE-SHOT-LIST.md` section 1 says the drive plays one cue for the whole
  film, `title_theme`, the A minor waltz, and its Q2 names this lane as the owner of the settlement.
  This card asked for a three section drive cue, so that is what was written, on the same grid and in
  the same key as the waltz, at the same 126 bpm. Both can be true: the waltz can stay the title card's
  cue and this can be the drive's, or this can be wired as `title_theme`'s second movement. gamedev
  decides, and until then nothing in the engine points at it.
- Not done, by scope: no edit to `data/audio.json`, `data/scenes/all.json`, art, or the engine; the
  rain and thunder cues the shot list asks for in Q2 are sound effects, not this cue; no NES ROM.
