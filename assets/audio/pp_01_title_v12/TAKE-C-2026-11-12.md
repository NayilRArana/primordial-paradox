## 2. What the take is

One 2 bar cell, the title leitmotif head then the answer:

* bar 1 `A4 B4 C5 D5 A5` (durations 2 2 2 2 4): steps up to D5, then the one leap of the cell,
  a fifth to the peak A5.
* bar 2 `A5 G5 E5 D5 C5 B4` (2 2 2 2 2 2): the whole fall by steps, ending on the leading tone
  so the next statement's A4 is the resolution.

11 notes, 10 intervals of which 8 are steps, exactly one leap, 13 semitones of range, an arch
contour on every return that is not the device. The leitmotif L (E D C B | B | E C D E | B G# A B)
is stated whole in bars 1 to 4 before the cell takes the cue: the melody notes of bars 1 to 3,
read back out of the MIDI, are E5 D5 C5 B4 | A4 B4 E5 C5 D5 E5 | B4 A4 G#4 A4 B4, which is all 13
L notes in order with one A4 pickup inserted before the second B4.

Every third return of the cell is varied: bars 9 to 10, 15 to 16, 21 to 22 and 27 to 28 hold the
peak a fifth lower (G5) and land the fall on the tonic instead of the leading tone.

The device, by `knowledge/MUSIC-DISSONANCE.md`: bars 13 to 14 and 23 to 24 hold A5 while the bass
moves to F (a suspension, the upper voice held while the bass moves), resolving down to G5 and E5.
That is the only chromatic motion a listener hears; the G#4 of the leitmotif's turn is the leading
tone of A minor, not an accidental.

Drums vary every 4 bars (three figures: `x...x...x...` kick on the one, a `x.....x.x...` kick on
the two, and a thinned `303030303030` hat figure in the last section) and every 8 bars (bar 8 of
each block takes the fill `x...x...x.xx` with a `....x...xxxx` snare). Every hit is inside the
real chip limits: the noise channel plays one pitch at a time and the dpcm line one sample at a
time, which is what the builder enforces with `check_mono`.
