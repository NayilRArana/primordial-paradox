## 3. What take D changes

Recipe: `tools/counterpoint/final_cues.py`, cue `spare4` (`spare_v4()`), slug
`pp_15b_credits_spare_v4`. The sheet is untouched: A major, 4/4, 144 bpm, 32 bars (53.333 s), and
the same delivered chord plan A E | D A | F#m E | D E, repeated.

Two edits, and nothing else. A bar by bar diff of the melody track against take C finds 17 bars
changed and all of them are these two edits:

1. The turn. Bar 2 was `C#5 B4 D5 C#5 E5 D5`; the `B4` to `D5` fourth is why 7 of 11 intervals
   were steps. It is now `C#5 B4 C#5 C#5 E5 D5`: the turn note is the step above `B4`, so 8 of 11
   intervals are steps (73 percent) and the one leap left in the motif is the fifth at the end of
   bar 1 (`E5` down to `B4`). The same move is applied to the dressed cell's bar 2
   (`C#5 E5 C#5 C#5 E5 E5`), so all fourteen statements stay one cell.
2. The rise. Bars 17 and 19 were `E5 A5 A5 B4 E5 B4` and bars 18 and 20 were
   `D5 B4 D5 B4 E5 D5`; between them the line turns nine times, so neither pair read as arch or
   pedal. They are now `C#5 D5 E5 F#5 A5 A5` over A, `B4 C#5 E5 F#5 G#5 G#5` over E, and
   `A5 F#5 E5 C#5 A4` falling home over A: rise to the peak, hold it, fall once.

Two gate findings had to be closed on the first build of the rise, and both were a semitone
against `pulse 1`'s arpeggio third on beat 3 of bars 19 and 20 (`A5` over `G#5`, `D5` over
`C#5`): CP-01 count 2, state REFUSED. Moving the E chord's peak to its own third (`G#5`) and the
A chord's fall through `C#5` took the count to 0 with the arch shape intact. The rejected first
rise is not kept on disk; the numbers are in this paragraph and the final file is the proof.

Unchanged from take C, and re-verified here: the H family statement of the leitmotif (one two bar
cell in a five semitone pedal, dressed in four units so the third return is 67 percent the same),
the drums varying every 4 and every 8 bars, the humanization inside the score (four value lead
velocity table, delayed vibrato on every held lead note, per block hat velocities, a CC11 swell at
each section head), and eight monophonic tracks inside the 2A03 plus VRC6 board.

## 6. The commit was refused, and why

`python guard/lane_commit.py --repo "<workspace>/Shared" --lane musician` staged the
six non-ignored paths (the MIDI, MP3 and WAV are gitignored in this project) and the pre-commit
hook passed the claim, dash, leak and syntax checks, then the gate refused:

    gate: primordial-paradox staged check: FAIL exit=1
    UNPAIRED IN COMMIT .../pp_15b_credits_spare_v4.ogg | the index project names no
    datafiles/streams/pp_15b_credits_spare_v4.ogg

That is the asset pairing rule, not a path mistake: a committed stream has to be named in
`game/PrimordialParadox.yyp`, and the project file has to be regenerated for this take in a
scratch worktree of HEAD plus these paths (`tools/sync_data.py`, `tools/sync_art.py --no-project`,
`tools/gen_project.py`) before it can travel in the same commit. The 45 minute budget ended
before that work could be run and verified, so nothing landed: take D is on disk, complete and
measured, and the commit is the next card's job.
