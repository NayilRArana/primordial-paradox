## 3. The edit: the ornament goes on the second note, and the gate decides

`tools/humanize.py` places its one mordent on a note of the motif's third return and offers three
placements (`--ornament-note first|second|none`). Take D ran it at the default, `first`, and that
pass puts the ornament on a pitch that changes which 2 bar segment the meter hears as the motif:
the best segment moves off bar 1 and its interval set is 45 percent steps. The same pass at
`second` keeps the motif at bar 1 and the heard take at 100, with all 194 of its notes, its 16
breaths, its 4 fills and its vibrato intact.

Measured on take E's own score, same humanize pass, three placements:

| ornament placement | heard hook | notes | weakest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| first (take D's) | 96 | 194 | steps over leaps (45 %) |
| **second (take E)** | **100** | **194** | motif coverage (full) |
| none | 100 | 191 | motif coverage (full) |

`none` also reaches 100 by dropping the grace note and the mordent, so it buys points by removing
humanity. `second` keeps every ornament the pass writes and still measures 100, so it is the take.
The script `build_pp_15b_v5.py` searches further than these three: with the heard take still short
of 100 it tries every allowed semitone move of every melody note inside the motif returns, keeps
only moves that raise the score and pass `tools/counterpoint/cue_gate.py`, and stops at the first
one that reaches 100. No repair was needed (`repair_report_v5.json`: `"applied": false`,
`"trials": 0`).

`tools/render_chip.py` on the heard MIDI, v2 voices (2A03 pulse 1, pulse 2, triangle, noise, DPCM

Loop seam from `pp_15b_credits_spare_v5_loop.json`:

| field | value |
| --- | --- |
| loop_bars | 32 |
| loop_start_sample | 0 |
| loop_end_sample | 2352002 |
| loop_seam_sample_difference | 0 |
| loop_edge_max_jump | 80 (of 32768) |
| fade | 5.0 ms raised cosine |
| peak / RMS before master | -1.00 / -16.25 dBFS |

The seam is the renderer's own three pass trim: the last sample of the loop and the first sample of
the next pass are the same value, and the largest jump across the join is 80 LSB, which is the 5 ms
fade, not a step in the music.

Master and delivery (`limit_master_v5.py`): fixed gain plus a look ahead limiter at a -2.6 dB
ceiling, which walked the delivered OGG to **-14.13 LUFS and -1.04 dBTP**, the same place take D's
delivery sits (-14.13 LUFS, -1.22 dBTP). A linear loudnorm pass could not do it: holding -1 dBTP
that way parked the cue at -15.20 LUFS, 1.2 LU quieter than every other cue in the family.
