## What the music does

One two bar cell, the harbor tide stated in steps rather than in the triad:

    bar 1 (rise):  A4 B4 C5 D5 E5 E5        stepwise rise to a held peak, the peak repeated
    bar 2 (fall):  E5 D5 B4 A4 E4 E4        stepwise fall, one leap (A4 to E4, 5 semitones)

Twelve notes, 8 of 11 intervals are steps, one leap, a repeated pitch and one turn, so every
return reads as an arch. The third unit of each four raises the peak to A5 and answers down
G5 E5 D5 C5 A4, the tide's own crest and fall, which is why the third return is 69 percent
the same and not a copy. Bars 31 and 32 are the cadence: the head held on the tonic, then one
more attack at step 12.

The chords are Am, F and Dm, with E or Am in the second bar of each unit. Section map, eight
bars each: squall (the cell, twice), gale (the cell with the harmony voice a third under the
lead), eye (the same cell, softer: no arpeggio sixteenths, softer hats, no snare), rolling
(the cell again at full weight). Drums: a different kick and hat figure in each four bar
block, a different one again in each eight bar block, and the eighth bar of every block takes
the fill.
